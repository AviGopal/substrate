## Discoverability convention

This is the same convention as chunk-00:

- A mechanism is discoverable if it is a registered resolver shape (the `config.ts` + `routes/impulses.ts` three-place rule), an activity row Thompson can select, or a concept in concept-db that the drafter recalls at prompt-build.
- In-process gates in `vessel-mitosis-cutover.ts` and `vessel-mitosis-evaluate.ts` are **not** discoverable today. For each of them I name the concept that should carry it.
- Runtime-state *files* (policies, leases, learning-mode state) are invisible to traces and the walk (law 1). For each I name the pool shape it should become.
- Fossils go to `archive/fossils/<area>/<name>/` in the super-repo, with a pointer to the last commit, and their rows are retired via the retire primitive (19ae84e). Nothing is deleted.
