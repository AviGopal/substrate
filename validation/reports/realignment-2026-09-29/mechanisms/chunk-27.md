## Discoverability and archive convention

- **Kept rows** stay discoverable as `activity` rows that Thompson selects. The resolvers they call are registered shapes in `config.ts` and `routes/impulses.ts`, advertised via discovery.
- **In-process gates** (mint dedup, reuse-before-mint) are not discoverable today. For each one, I name the concept that should carry it so the drafter and detectors can cite it.
- **Fossil rows** are retired with the retire primitive (`activity.retired`, 19ae84e). Retirement is honoured: only 2 retired rows ran in the window. Their definitions are archived as JSON under `archive/fossils/activity-pool/<family>/` in the super-repo, with the last vpm counters and the id of the commit that minted them.
- **Fossil code and schema** are tombstoned under `archive/fossils/<vessel>/<name>`.
- Nothing is deleted.
