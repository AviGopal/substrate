## Discoverability convention used below

Same convention as chunk-00. A mechanism is discoverable if one of these holds:

- it is a **registered shape** advertised through discovery;
- it is an **activity row** Thompson can select;
- it is a **concept** the drafter and walk recall (`[walk-concepts] consulted concept-db via discovery` shows the walk already recalls concepts by term).

Most of goal-host's mechanisms are in-process branches of `runGoalAsPoolWalk` (4,566 lines), `runGoalWithRecoveryInner` (2,325) or `handleRunGoal` (1,060). None of these is discoverable. **Node-local JSONL stores** (failure memory, reached-command bank, verifier recipes) are also unreachable over p2p: node 2 cannot see what node 1 learned. For each kept mechanism the table names the shape or concept that should carry it.

**Fossil archive.** Fossils go to `archive/fossils/goal-host-vessel/<name>/` in the super-repo, as a code tombstone plus the last-commit pointer. Their activity and gap rows are retired through the retire primitive (19ae84e). Nothing is deleted.
