## Discoverability convention used below

A mechanism is discoverable when a walk or detector can find it by shape. That means one of three things:

- a `case '<shape>'` arm in `routes/impulses.ts` plus an advertisement in `config.ts`, reachable via discovery;
- an activity row that Thompson can select;
- a concept in concept-db that the drafter recalls at prompt-build.

The recurring pattern in this vessel is that most learning machinery is **bare REST**, not shapes. Examples: `/recommend`, `/execution-traces/reach`, `/composition`, `/decision-calibration`, `/templates/auto-promote`, `/conservation-audit-emit`. Callers pin `${ACTIVITY_API_ENDPOINT}/v2/activities/...`. A walk cannot find these, and a spoke only reaches them through an env-pinned URL. For kept REST mechanisms I name the shape that should front them.

Fossils archive under `archive/fossils/activity-api/<name>/` in the super-repo: a code tombstone plus a pointer to the last commit. Their DB tables get an export plus a `REMOVE TABLE` migration applied on unit start (CLAUDE.md: never hand-edit the DB). Activity rows are retired via the retire primitive (19ae84e). Nothing is deleted outright.
