## Cross-cutting findings for this area

1. **Status is not evidence on this plane.** Every timer reports `Result=success`, including:
   - memory-budget-check (exit 1 mapped to success)
   - the watchdog (HTTP 200 while the stall grows)
   - pull-sync (clean over a damaged tree)
   - surgical-gap-scan (0 emitted)
   - self-repair-operational (0 repaired)

   Rule for the kept ones: a tick's success must be computed from its effect (a marker moved, rows emitted, the tree matches HEAD), not from exit status.
2. **Detection without disposition is the recurrence engine.** model-reality-audit (11 days), joint-liveness (`severed-joint-*` since 09-28), learning-loop-selftest (RED since 09-19) and trace-store-health (over cap every tick) re-detect forever, because their gaps carry no `edit_site` and nothing counts the run length. Reviving `identical-failure-run-tick` (row 1) is the missing half. Pairing it with `edit_site` on detector-born gaps is the other half.
3. **Hub-only detectors that open SurrealDB directly fail off-hub.** This covers joint-liveness, validator-liveness and compose-drift. Pull-sync then auto-enabled them on node 2, and they were hand-masked there. That is the node-locality class, three instances deep. The fix is resolve-by-shape plus a profile-aware enable, not masks.
4. **Timer plane versus the rhythm conductor.** The conductor that law 5 prescribes exists and idles, with no per-family reasons. The ~40 static timers are the real cadence. Consolidation keeps only the bootstrap / liveness tier as units and moves behaviour one family at a time.
5. **Fossils to delete from `scripts/substrate/`:**
   - `operator-goal-generator.ts`
   - `operator-goal-signal.ts`
   - `performance-status.ts`
   - `autonomy-status.ts`
   - `self-development-trend.ts` (plus unit)
   - `surgical-gap-scan.ts` (plus unit)
   - `warn-baseline-drift.sh`
   - the runtime-drift repair branch
   - the watchdog drop-ins
   - all `.js`, `.d.ts` and `.map` residue beside them

   The archive is git history. The seeder and pull-sync `cp -f` into `/workspace/active-scripts` never prune, so deleted scripts also have to be pruned from that runtime copy.
