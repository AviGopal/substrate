## Cross-cutting observations for this area

1. **Three "dormant" readings in the inputs were produced by a mislabelled health field.** `/health.upkeep.enabled` reports `DENSE_BACKFILL_ENABLED`. Upkeep is running; it is **untraced** (401), which is a different defect with a different fix. This is the MEMORY law "a channel's own reporting is not evidence about it" recurring inside concept-db's own telemetry.
2. **Transport asymmetry is the single root behind A, B and C.** Reads use discovery `/v2/impulses/resolve`. Writes, grading and `consultPrinciples` use a hardcoded `127.0.0.1:8260` or unmounted routes. One change fixes the class: route every concept-db call from dev-vessel by shape through discovery. That covers the decentralisation principle too: absence of concept-db on node 2 is not absence.
3. **Recall (D) is the load-bearing hole under A, C, K and O.** Ten patches have landed on the ladder since 08-10. The live positive control (term `signature`, 0 hits over 76k matching rows) says the next step is an instrumented probe at the index, not an 11th ladder patch.
4. **Write-only residue** accounts for G (24k audit impulses), L (22k edges), and the M schema with no reader. The rule for this area: nothing gets revived without its reader landing first.
