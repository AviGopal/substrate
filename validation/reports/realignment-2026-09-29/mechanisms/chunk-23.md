## Cross-cutting findings for this area

1. **The same issue recurs for the same reason, at the fixed point.**
   - Registration contracts: the three `resolve_endpoint` formats, empty shapes for consumers, and no declared `distribution_policy`. None of these is normalized at `/register`, so every consumer re-derives them. The result: 9 unguarded URL joiners, 4 `llmCall` copies, 3 producer pickers, and 3 loopback rules.
   - The single highest-leverage edit set in this area is `/register`. Normalize the endpoint, accept consumers, and default and require a policy for stateful shapes.
2. **Silent peer outcomes are why federation keeps "working" on paper.**
   - Fan-out works (node 2 rows arrive through `:26100`). A peer that rejects our key (`syzygy.host:18100`, 401) is indistinguishable from no peer.
   - The gap about exactly this has produced 204 rows since 09-20 without a landing. This is the chunk's clearest case of narrowing churn standing in for a one-line fix.
3. **Env-gated behavior (law 1) remains in the join path:** `PEER_DISCOVERY_ENDPOINTS`, `HUB_API_KEY`, `MAX_PEER_DEPTH`, `RELAY_MULTIADDR`, `HUB_DISCOVERY_URL`, `PEER_FANOUT_MODE`. `gap-mu82wqoh` shows the cost: a reboot de-federates. The `substrateBootstrap` shape exists and should be the reader's source.
4. **Nothing new needs minting in this area.** Every recommended change is an edit to an existing seam (`/register`, `forwardToPeers`, `/bootstrap`, the `reachable-from` package, `buildDiscoveryProxyResolver`) or a declaration by existing vessels.
