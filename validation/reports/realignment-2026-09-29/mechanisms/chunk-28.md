## Discoverability convention

This is the same convention as chunk-00 and chunk-10:

- A mechanism is discoverable if it is a registered resolver shape (the `config.ts` + `routes/impulses.ts` rule), an activity row Thompson can select, a timer with a journal the observers read, or a concept in concept-db that the drafter recalls at prompt-build.
- Fossil *tables* are not dropped by hand (CLAUDE.md: never hand-edit the DB; schema changes are migrations). They are archived by a migration that exports them to `archive/fossils/database/<table>/` (NDJSON plus the defining migration's filename) and then `REMOVE TABLE`. Their code writers are deleted in the same traced goal.
- Fossil *activity rows* are retired through the retire primitive (`19ae84e`), never deleted.

## Cross-class notes

The same reason keeps recurring, and that is what the realignment should fix:

1. **A write that silently does nothing.** #5, #9, #3 and #4's history (the edge write that "never landed" for weeks) share one root: `query()` does not check statement status (`src/db/surreal.ts`, noted in its own docstring since 08-21 and still unfixed). The write-read-mismatch class keeps coming back through this one seam. Fixing it once at the seam (#5) turns every row in #9 from a silent zero into a visible error or a traced gap. Do this before any per-table work.
2. **Tables outliving their writers, and writers outliving their tables.** #3, #10 and #11 are schema and code that nothing retires. The system has a retire primitive for *activities* (`19ae84e`) but none for *tables*. The missing generator is a detector that pairs `INFO FOR DB` table row counts and last-write times with a grep of live writers and readers, and files a gap per mismatch. That is what this chunk did by hand (law 6: if I did it by hand, the missing generator is the gap).
3. **Unbounded children of capped parents.** #2 repeats the trace-store growth class under a new table name. Cap by cascade, not per table.
