**Frame.** This area is the scheduling and deploy layer. Almost nothing in it is an activity. It is shell and TS run by systemd timers, so selection cannot choose it, traces do not grade it, and discovery cannot find it (law 2). The CLAUDE.md bootstrap-tier exception covers the Makefile, entrypoint, gen-env, unit rendering, secrets/readiness helpers, the seeders, and the two liveness watchdogs. It does **not** cover the ~30 detector ticks. For those, "keep" means keep the *detection* and move it to a shape-addressed activity whose gap carries an `edit_site`. Three root causes recur across this chunk, and each verdict below is tagged with the ones it shows:
- **(A) liveness certified by the wrong signal.** Exit 0, HTTP 200 or `is-active` is taken to mean the unit worked.
- **(B) detection without an address.** The detector refreshes a gap every tick with an empty `edit_site`, so nothing can close it.
- **(C) node-local addressing.** Detectors open `127.0.0.1:8000/sql` or `/workspace/metrics/*` directly instead of resolving by shape. They fail on a spoke and get masked (node-locality).
