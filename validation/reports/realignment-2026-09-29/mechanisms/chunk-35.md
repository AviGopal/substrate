## Discoverability convention

"Pool records" are `poolImpulse` rows, and `poolImpulse` is served by development-vessel. A record is discoverable when three things hold:
1. its shape is resolvable by shape through discovery. That is `resolve_impulse({shape:"poolImpulse", pointer:{type:"<shape>"}})`. Resolving `{type:autonomyScope}` directly at dev-vessel returns "Unknown pointer type" by design (openspec-7).
2. a concept in concept-db names the shape, its reader and its invariant, so the drafter and detectors can cite it.
3. it exists on **every node that runs its reader**.

The third condition is the recurring failure in this chunk. Records are hand-mirrored per node, and absence on one node silently changes behaviour there (law 11; the user's "absence in one place is not absence"). The fix for the whole area is one mechanism: pool records that carry fleet policy replicate over the federation (or are resolved from the hub by shape), and a reader that cannot find its record fails closed and gaps on itself. Per-node mirroring should not be the fix.

Fossils go to `archive/fossils/pool-records/<name>` in the super-repo: a tombstone README with the last commit and the evidence. Their activity rows are retired via the retire primitive (`19ae84e`). Nothing is deleted.

## Cross-cutting findings for the synthesis

1. **Policy records are per-node and hand-mirrored.** `autonomyScope` and `spendEnvelope` were injected on both nodes by `operator:claude-avi`. `jointBinding` exists only on node 1, and node 2's detector is `failed`. This is the same issue recurring for the same reason: node-locality and law 11. Fleet policy must be resolved by shape from where it lives (the hub), or federated, and its readers must fail closed and gap on absence.
2. **Detectors fire, and nothing closes.** 5 severed-joint gaps are open and 0 closed. The watchdog has restarted 5,097+ times. The discarded-landing detector writes a file no one reads. In every case the detector exists, but the reader and the disposition that would act on it do not (law 7; openspec-10 "detectors built without a reader").
3. **Containment and the watchdog fight each other.** Containment admits 0 of 851 by design, and the watchdog reads that as a stall and restarts every few minutes. Both are correct in isolation. What is missing is a shared shape that says "contained, not stalled".
4. **Operator-authored policy oscillates.** `autonomyScope` changed 4 times in 48 h, and the `spendEnvelope` pause and reopen were operator decisions. These are dispositions the system should learn from landing outcomes, with the operator as a resolver (law 13), not as the only author.
