## Cross-cutting findings for the synthesis

1. **identity-vessel is the single validator (CLAUDE.md), yet it is not registered in discovery** (`registered:false`). Every auth caller pins an endpoint. This is the same "writer pins a peer" class as the `:8270` escalations. Registering it is the prerequisite for I3 to work over p2p.
2. **Most of the identity journal is noise from one fossil (I5), and most of its trace volume is reason-less failures (I4).** Neither has a reader. Both are write-only emitters (principle: every write-only shape needs a named reader or a TTL).
3. **Tenant isolation is durable only by hand (I6).** It is enforced on 3 tables, not on `api_key`/`key_session`, and it is bypassed by the vessel's own root connection.
