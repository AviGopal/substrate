## Discoverability convention

This follows chunk-00. A mechanism is discoverable when a walk or detector can find it by shape. That means one of:

- a registered resolver shape advertised through discovery;
- an activity row that Thompson can select;
- a concept in concept-db that the drafter or walk recalls.

In-process helpers are not discoverable today. For those I name the shape they should be extracted to, or the concept that should carry them.

Fossils go to `archive/fossils/goal-host-vessel/<name>` in the super-repo, as a code tombstone plus a pointer to the last commit. Their rows are retired through the retire primitive (`19ae84e`). Nothing is deleted.
