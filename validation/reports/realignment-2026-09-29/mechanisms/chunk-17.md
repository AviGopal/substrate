## Discoverability convention

A mechanism is **discoverable** when at least one of these holds:

- It is a shape in `src/config.ts` plus `routes/impulses.ts`, advertised through discovery.
- It is an activity row that Thompson can select.
- It is a concept in concept-db that the drafter recalls at prompt-build.
- It is a `substrate_tuning_param` row that the selector reads at use time (the law-1 seam).

Env flags and `setInterval` loops in `index.ts` are **not discoverable**. For each of those I name the tuning row or rhythm family that should carry it.

Fossils are archived under `archive/fossils/activity-api/<name>/`, as a code tombstone plus the last commit hash, and they get a tombstone concept in concept-db. Their activity rows are retired with the retire primitive (19ae84e) and a stated `retired_reason`.
