## Discoverability convention used below

A mechanism is "discoverable" when a walk or a detector can find it by shape. That means one of three things:

- a registered resolver shape in `config.ts` and `routes/impulses.ts` (the three-place rule), advertised via discovery;
- a `development-vessel:*` activity row that Thompson can select;
- a concept in concept-db that the drafter recalls at prompt-build.

Pure in-process helpers (gates inside `feature-compose.ts`, `gap-to-feature.ts`, `vessel-mitosis-cutover.ts`) are not discoverable today. For those I name the concept that should carry them, so the drafter and detectors can cite them.

Fossils are archived under `archive/fossils/development-vessel/<name>` in the super-repo (a code tombstone plus a pointer to its last commit), and their rows are retired via the retire primitive (19ae84e). Nothing is deleted.
