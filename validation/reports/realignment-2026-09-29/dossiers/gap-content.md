**One line.** A gap enters the store as prose. It has no edit site, a wrong one, or one copied from the victim file. It has no machine-checkable predicate, or one that can never read "present". Often its premise is already false. So nothing downstream can act on it, verify a landing against it, or learn from it. Since June this has been compensated at five or more seams *after* the gap is filed: admission, the picker, lifecycle scans, decomposition and closure sweeps. The gap write itself (`substrateGap_write`) has never required what those seams need. On 2026-09-29, 1,622 of 1,817 open gaps (89%) are `falsifier:none`, only 100 (5.5%) have an edit site plus a class1/class2 check, and auto-pick admission is **863 candidates → 0 admitted on both nodes**.

## 1. What the class actually is

The records name the symptom: "gaps born without an edit site or falsifier". They trace back to one shared mechanism with two halves.

1. **The filer holds the evidence and throws it away.** Every detector that files a gap has just measured something: a query result, a field, a count, a file it read, a log signature. The write carries a *summary sentence* about that measurement, not the measurement itself. `classifyFalsifier` (`development-vessel/src/resolvers/substrate-gap.ts:485`, called at `:1045`) then honestly stamps `none`, because prose is not a predicate. The write succeeds, and the live log line reads `[gap-falsifier] created <id>: falsifier=none` (seen 09-29 03:33:50 for `gap-detailed-decomposition-report`).
2. **Every consumer re-derives what the filer dropped, from text, later.** Localization, premise, predicate and site are reconstructed downstream by regex or LLM over the summary: goal-file-resolution, `admitActionableGaps`, `gap_falsify`, the decomposer, the recommit/narrowing minters and the drafter. Each reconstruction is lossy. Some are wrong in systematic ways: the most-cited path is the *victim* file (09-15), a 400-char slice cuts a path mid-extension (09-10), and a predicate derived after landing always reads present (08-31). Lineage minters copy the prose forward, so the error propagates to children.

The measured consequence (memory-10, 09-13): the substrate **applies** a specified change about 80% of the time (operator specs 12/15) and **derives** one from a problem statement about 2.5% of the time (autonomous code-edit 9/364; 0/194 earlier). The bottleneck is gap content, not drafting.

## 3. Root causes

1. **The write seam has no content contract.** `substrateGap_write` accepts any summary, any or no site, a placeholder string as id or status, and any predicate or none. It stamps `falsifier:none` and returns success. Law 1 says behaviour is steered by shaped impulses. The gap *is* the shaped impulse that steers the whole repair lane, and its shape is unconstrained where it matters.
2. **Detectors emit categories and prose, not their own measurement.** The detector that decided "divergence_count > 0" or "occurrence_count ≥ N" or "0 rows in window" already holds a class-2 predicate (shape plus field plus polarity). It writes an English sentence instead. The working exceptions (`trace_failure_pattern_report` with `reason_contains`, `test_suite requested_not_passing`, `producer_count`, federation-probe `fed:*`, `614de014`) prove the detector-side form works.
3. **Everything downstream re-derives from text, after birth, at the wrong time.** Examples: the localizer (most-cited path = victim), slicing (400 chars), and predicate derivation at land time (`8a5223c`: can never read absent). Each is a separate lossy reconstruction with its own bug history.
4. **Lineage copies instead of enriching.** Retries fork new ids (`-narrowed`, `recommit-`, `recommit-recommit-`, `-step-N`). Children inherit the pre-correction site and none of the check. Re-detection after a store reset starts with `reopen_count 0` and no memory of prior attempts.
5. **The store cannot remember.** The single JSON file loses writes under concurrency (9 of 22 lost while reporting `updated`) and collapsed on 09-09 and again on 09-18, erasing all history before 09-18. Commit bodies carry only ids. So the recurrence that law 7 (durability) needs is invisible, and every "first" claim is unfalsifiable against history.
6. **Admission compensates, so starvation reads as correctness.** Twelve or more gates correctly refuse unactionable gaps. The result is 0/863 admitted. The gate is doing its job, and the supply that feeds it is broken.

## 5. Why it recurs: the missing shared capability

**Missing capability: a gap-birth contract at the one write seam every filer already uses.**

Every filer uses one write seam: `substrateGap_write` → `substrate-gap.ts` (`classifyFalsifier` :485, :1045), held on the gap-store holder and forwarded from spokes by `forwardToGapStore`. A contract there, read as a shape (law 1) rather than hard-coded, would turn every filing into one of three outcomes:

1. **Actionable gap.** The filer supplies (a) an `edit_site` that **exists on the owning node's push clone** at write time, and (b) a class1 or class2 predicate that the seam **evaluates once at birth and that reads "present"** (the defect is observable now). The contract rejects a predicate that reads absent at birth (born closable, arming-guard class) or cannot be evaluated (`unresolvable`). For detectors the predicate is the detector's *own* measurement handed over as data: shape + field + polarity + threshold. The detector already computed it, so nothing is re-derived from prose, and nothing is derived at land time (the `8a5223c` trap).
2. **Observation, not gap.** The filer supplies neither. The write is recorded as a demand-counted observation, keyed by `gapClassKey` with id discriminators stripped. It becomes a gap only when demand recurs (the `4361247` rule, generalized from capability gaps to every filer). At that point it is routed to an *investigate/decompose* goal (the boredom `12bb26a` form) whose output is a site plus a predicate written back to the parent. No new id is minted.
3. **Enrichment of an existing gap.** Retries, narrowing, decomposition, recommit and re-detection all write **into the parent**: lessons, a corrected site, a sharper predicate. They do not fork. Recurrence is then a counter on the parent that survives, and durability becomes measurable.

The contract also refuses unrendered `{{…}}` placeholders and any summary under a minimum length, and it records `filer` (detector or activity id) so that each family's birth quality becomes a per-detector posterior the system reads. The system then learns *which detectors produce actionable gaps* (law 7's "learned disposition") instead of the operator hand-closing floods every era.

**Why every earlier attempt fell short.** Each fix was applied at a *consumer* seam, after birth, and compensated for one symptom: the picker (`4e5a603`, `7d48a27`), admission (`76f44ca`, `ea2476c`, `c23b601`, `bea12ec`, `bb51374`, `d7ec192`), lifecycle (`fbf7433`, `gap_falsify`), decomposition (`2de0ae8`), the drafter (`19bbb8b`, `9f222fb`), lineage (`206f055b`, `a57dc30`), or a single generator (`818dcfd`, `7868111`, `789853a`, `4361247`, `614de014`). New generators keep appearing: this week `poison-*`, `model-reality-phantom-failure-*`, `wasted-cycle-*` and `model-opportunity-*`. Each is born unactionable, because the only rule that would stop that lives downstream. The one attempt at the write seam (`be26a6b`, 08-30) derived the predicate *from text at land time* instead of taking it *from the detector at detection time*. It failed for that reason and was withdrawn, not reworked. SPEC-REVIEW (09-27) found the contract "unspecified anywhere" across 97 changes.

This fits the user's operating model. The walk can route around a siteless gap, and `12bb26a` plus decomposition are that route-around. But nothing **encapsulates the evidence** that the route-around keeps recurring for the same reason. The gap store should be the place where that evidence accumulates, and it drops the evidence at the door.

## 8. Retire condition (measurable, checked continuously, hard to game)

The class is retired when **all** of the following hold over a trailing 7-day window. The measurement must be computed by a system activity that writes a shaped report (not an operator query), on the gap-store holder, including gaps forwarded from every node:

1. **Birth quality.** Of gaps born with `source ≠ human_reported/operator_*`, **≥ 80%** are either (a) *actionable*: the `edit_site` exists on the owning node's push clone at birth, **and** a class1/class2 predicate was evaluated at birth and read `present`; or (b) recorded as a demand-counted observation with `demand_count = 1`, not an open gap. Today (a) is at most 12 of 118 substrate-detected births in 24 h (≤10%; 9 class1 + 3 class2, before the birth-time present check that does not yet exist), and (b) covers capability gaps only.
2. **No unactionable open gap from a detector.** **0** open gaps per detector family with `falsifier ∈ {none, unresolvable}` and age > 24 h, unless routed to an investigate goal that wrote back to the *same id* within 48 h. Today: 14 named detector families (reach-gap, docs-drift, systematic-failure, template-input-lint, pull-sync, precondition-rejection, unaccounted-landing, runtime-drift, runtime-source-truncated, model-reality, model-opportunity, poison, poisson, severed-joint) are 0% actionable.
3. **Supply reaches the lane.** On each node, auto-pick admission admits **≥ 1 candidate on ≥ 50% of passes**, with `needs_information` at ≤ 10% of candidates. Today: 0/863 on both nodes, and `needs_information` is 39% (n1) and 51% (n2).
4. **Closure by measurement.** ≥ 50% of gaps closed in the window close with a `predicate_verified_*`/`landed_verified` basis where the predicate read `present` before the landing and `absent` after, with **no** flip back to open within 7 days. That last clause guards against the flapping `self-fact-divergence` pattern.
5. **Durability is measurable and clean.** The store keeps every gap id (no collapse against the high-water mark), and **0** gaps are re-filed under a new id with the same `edit_site` and predicate within 14 days. Lineage children count as forks unless they are writes into the parent.
6. **Autonomy split.** Items 1–4 hold for gaps whose filer and landing are *not* operator-directed. That means filtering out `directed:true` and route-edit exact-edit dispatches, so the operator lane cannot satisfy the condition.

Falsifier for "retired": inject a known synthetic defect into a sandbox store through a fresh detector that emits only prose. The contract must record it as an observation, not an open gap. A second injection with the detector's measurement attached must be born actionable, with the predicate reading present.
