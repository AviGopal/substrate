## The rubric

Every seam is judged on three INDEPENDENT conditions, never collapsed:

1. **Agreement** — the key/shape/type/value-form the producer writes is what the
   consumer reads. Statically checkable. Value-form counts as much as name.
2. **Liveness** — data is actually crossing now, counted at the CONSUMING side.
   "The code path exists" is not liveness; a row from a dead batch job is not
   liveness.
3. **Visibility** — if flow stopped, something says so. `NEGATIVE` means a check
   would PASS while the seam is dead — the worst case, and the one that concealed
   the reconcile job for weeks.
