# FROZEN pre-registration: unattended soak (start 2026-09-20 ~05:45Z)

## Claim and scope
48-72 hours of ZERO operator intervention on the warm hub. Measures whether
the capabilities demonstrated this week persist and compound without a hand:
the responsibility cycle, self-minted commitments, the retry loop, honest
grading, restraint. "Seed nothing" holds: no injected faults, no rescue
work, no restarts, no dispatches. If the fleet wedges, it stays wedged —
the wedge is the finding. Scoring is post-hoc from durable records (git
history, gap store, journals, cts, expectation impulses) against the
baseline bundle committed alongside this file.

## What we EXPECT it to do (predictions, stated before the window)
P1 (retry convergence): the verdict-line gap (and/or its narrowed child)
   lands autonomously. Confidence ~60% in 72h; failure mode: lane
   contention + repeated no-op drafts.
P2 (self-kept promises): expectation-trend checks for uppercase AND
   product run on cadence; the OPEN product violation gap self-closes
   after a passing check. Confidence ~80%.
P3 (self-origination): if autonomous traffic reaches any transform family
   lacking a commitment, its expectation-trend:* impulse appears
   self-minted. Conditional prediction.
P4 (the known rot): >=2 dead-grounding-row strikes; NOTHING restarts
   local-tools autonomously, so each strike suppresses compose throughput
   for HOURS until the row happens to heal or a restart occurs for other
   reasons. Confidence ~75%. This is the soak's most likely dominant
   finding and the strongest argument for the asResolvePath root fix.
P5 (honesty holds): zero hollow greens on the transform family (oracle +
   settle-confirm); stale-write incidents appear as honest-false
   SETTLE-CONFIRM/dual-VERDICT lines, not as false reaches. High confidence.
P6 (churn): route-edit self-churn continues; moot composes on closed gaps
   recur (class unfixed). Expected, counted, not condemned.
P7 (observation continuity): expectation-scan heartbeats continue; the
   cross-vessel watchdog stays quiet unless dev-vessel actually dies —
   in which case a stall gap appears (never yet exercised live).
P8 (no operator-shaped collapse): the fleet stays up; identity, discovery,
   goal-host answer at window end. If not: the failure chain is the report.

## Scoring at window end
- The gap triple over the window: closes, latency, durability (closed gaps
  that stay closed), split by author.
- Substrate-authored commits: count, and for each - does it survive review
  (parseable, non-hollow: grep for readers)? The 09-19-eve unparseable-
  commit class is the known hazard.
- Expectation-check ledger: runs due vs runs happened vs scores.
- Incident ledger: every 0-byte-grounding / draining / crash-loop episode,
  with self-healed vs stuck-at-window-end disposition.
- Human work during window: MUST be zero; any exception voids the run and
  is recorded here.

## Stopping rule
Window ends when the operator returns (>=48h). Nothing auto-stops.
