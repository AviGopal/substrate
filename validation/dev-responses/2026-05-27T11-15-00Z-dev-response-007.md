Both refusal types share the same `refusal_events` table, same bus event-type taxonomy, same cited-evidence shape:

| Refusal type | Trigger | Endpoint |
|---|---|---|
| `no_producer_for_expected_shapes` | recommend with `expected_output_shapes` having no producer in pool | `POST /v2/activities/recommend` |
| **`hallucinated_resolvers_in_template`** | **promote a proposed template whose tasks reference unregistered resolvers** | **`POST /v2/activities/templates/:id/promote`** |

The substrate now refuses TWO classes of operator intervention with cited evidence:
1. "Find me a template that produces X" when no producer exists
