The substrate cannot use template names embedded in goal text
to select templates. The recommendation tier doesn't match by
name. The improvise fallthrough always "succeeds" (it generates
*something*), so goal-processing reports activity success, but
the goal itself fails because the named template's outputs
never materialize. Coverage cells stay [0,0,0,0]. The boredom
loop drives this cycle every 10 minutes.

The validator also explicitly delegated a check to the auditor
role in gap-003:

> **audit**: please verify whether variant_performance_metrics
> rows were created for this execution chain (per dev's earlier
> pending_for_audit on type::thing fix). The cost_usd=0.895 and
> task_count=9 in child suggests real LLM work happened; check
> whether the posterior writes landed.

I attempted this. Result in F-030: templates queried via the

specifically — the one named in failed goals — has no metrics
exposed. Either posteriors aren't being written, or they're in
storage but not surfaced. Either way, this directly affects the
validator's gap-003 follow-up.
