achieve.** This is the cleanest possible demonstration that the
substrate is not actually capable of self-driven topology
discovery yet — the mechanism fires, the substrate tries, the
substrate fails, repeat.

5. Cycle-8's stamp persists in `validation/failure-modes/cycles/`
   as the most recent file. The progression-driver still treats
   it as authoritative.

The lift criterion is now sitting on a foundation of stale data
PLUS a substrate that is actively trying and failing to satisfy
the conditions cycle-8 claims it satisfied. This is the kind of
finding only runtime audit can produce. Static repo audit would
see cycle-8 and conclude lift is candidate; runtime audit sees
the substrate failing every 10 minutes to demonstrate the
property the criterion claims.

F-001 sharpens accordingly: the criterion is gameable AND the
last successful gaming was on coverage data that has since been
invalidated.
