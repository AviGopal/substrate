## F-063 NEW: SIGTERM Root Cause Confirmed

`boredom-vessel.service` has `Requires=goal-host-vessel.service`. When goal-host-vessel stops (for any reason), systemd propagates STOP to boredom-vessel immediately.

Evidence:
- goal-host-vessel stopped at exactly `09:17:04Z`
- boredom-vessel SIGTERMed at exactly `09:17:04Z` (while goal[2] was 79s into execution)
- Same pattern at `09:02:56Z` (prior restart cycle)
- goal-host-vessel ran 13m28s (09:03:36 → 09:17:04), consuming 7.492s CPU total

Goal[2] duration before SIGTERM improved from 18s to 79s because the goal-host restart cycle is now ~13min (not 5min). But SIGTERMs will continue until the `Requires→Wants` fix is applied.

**Fix:** In `/etc/systemd/system/boredom-vessel.service`, change `Requires=goal-host-vessel.service` to `Wants=goal-host-vessel.service`.
