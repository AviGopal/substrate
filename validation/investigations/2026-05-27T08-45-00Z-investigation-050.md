## Summary

The operator-dispatched probe ("fill out the Disrupt 2026 Startup Battlefield 200 application") surfaced a real substrate gap: the selector silently fell back to the highest-α template (`probe-reachable-unlearned`) when no producer existed for the requested output shapes (`answeredApplication`, `questionImpulse`, `answerImpulse`). The substrate succeeded confidently at the wrong task.

This iteration closes the gap with a refusal guard in `activity-api`'s `/v2/activities/recommend`. The substrate now **refuses with cited evidence** when no producer exists, rather than confidently dispatching the wrong template. This is the IAL §27.S.6 push-away closure property — the first substrate-side refusal mechanism to ship.

## Specification & implementation

**Trigger condition:** `expected_output_shapes.length > 0` AND no template in `validTemplates` (the full pre-ranking pool) has an output shape intersecting the requested set.

**Important:** checks the FULL candidate pool, not just the top-N `finalRecommendations` slice. Refusal means "no producer exists in the catalogue", not "the top-ranked candidates happen not to match." When a producer exists but isn't top-ranked, the recommend path proceeds normally (ranking issue is separate from refusal logic).

**Response shape changes (backward-compatible):**
- `recommendations: []` (existing throw-on-empty path in goal-host fires)
- `fallback_tier: "refused"` (new tier value)
- `refusal: {...}` (new optional field with structured rationale)
- `decision_record.rationale_tier: "refused"` (new tier value)

**Implementation:** `repos/metabob-activity-api/src/routes/activities.ts` — refusal block inserted between final-ranking and decision-record construction. ~70 lines added. Typechecked clean.
