## Operational takeaways

For the standing loop:

1. **Every future LIFT CANDIDATE stamp is suspect until
   externally anchored.** The investigator should treat such
   stamps as findings until `lift-criterion-hardening`'s external
   anchors (held-out evals, adversarial probes, CI agreement,
   chain-stall detection) are shipping AND gating.
2. **Verify timestamps.** Any proposal carrying
   `1970-01-01T00:00:00.000Z` is itself a finding. Any report
   with that timestamp is a finding about the report.
3. **Verify provenance.** Until vessel credentials can sign
   proposals (post-`substrate-identity-resolution`), the
   `authored_by` field is a string anyone can write. Treat it
   accordingly.
4. **Watch for substrate confusion.** Lift stamps issued against
   one substrate (canary) while the operational direction
   targets another (local container) are evidence of state
   reconciliation gaps.
5. **Track the proposal-to-DEV ratio.** 21 proposals drafted on
   one day is fine if they reach DEV at a corresponding pace; it's
   not fine if they accumulate as aspirational paperwork.
