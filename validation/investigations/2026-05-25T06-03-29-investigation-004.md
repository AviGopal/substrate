### What Remains Broken: F-037/F-043 (Thompson Disconnect)

Validator iter-16 also confirmed: **"+5 topology failures, zero Thompson
movement."** Despite 8 vessels running, boredom firing, and composition
chains populated, Thompson posteriors on coverage-tick and topology
templates remain at α=β=1.

`GoalHost` → `goal-host-vessel` → activity-api trace write) correctly
writes `activity_execution_traces` records, correctly populates
`composition_chain`, but **does not call the Thompson update code path**.

- `POST /v2/activities/execution-traces` with `success` field, OR

The new path writes traces but skips the feedback/posterior-update call.
This is the "Thompson bypassed" finding from validator iter-13 (`efa25a46`).

This remains the single highest-leverage unfixed item. 98+ coverage-tick
executions, 13+ failures, all yielding α=1, β=1.
