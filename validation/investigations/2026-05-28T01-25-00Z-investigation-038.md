## What the autonomous promoter does correctly

- **Strips Jeffreys prior before threshold** — `empirical_samples = (α-1)+(β-1)` means the gate measures evidence, not initial prior. This is mathematically right.
- **Audit trail is durable** — every decision writes to `promote_gate_evaluations` AND emits a WebSocket bus event. Closure-audit can inspect.
- **Two-pool partition on recommend** — proposed templates enter the *exploration pool* (per recommend handler change in same commit), so they CAN be selected to accumulate evidence. Without this, proposed templates would never get the samples they need to be promotion-eligible.
