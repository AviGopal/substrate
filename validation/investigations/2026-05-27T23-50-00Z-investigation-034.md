# Investigation 034 — Topology audit surfaces foundational Thompson measurement integrity issue

Subagent #16 (transcript: `/tmp/.../abbde1037f6dee377.output`) performed the operational catalog + topology audit the operator directed. The catalog and connectivity observations are useful but **secondary to a finding the audit cycles have been operating around without noticing**: Thompson posterior α-values are growing on SELECTION rather than terminal SUCCESS. This invalidates every measurement the iter-022→033 arc has interpreted through the posterior layer.

## The headline finding

Three high-execution discovery probes have α growth disproportionate to success counts:

| Template | α | β | total_executions | successful_executions | success_rate |
|----------|---|---|------------------|----------------------|--------------|
| `create-shape-provider-goal` | **832** | 84 | 823 | **0** | 0.00 |
| `probe-reachable-unlearned` | 31 | 4 | 30 | 0 | 0.00 |
| `probe-untraversed-edge` | 16 | 2 | 15 | 0 | 0.00 |
| `forge-vessel-for-shape` | 1 | 11 | 8 | 0 | 0.00 |

**This is structurally impossible under correct Thompson semantics.** α should only increment on terminal success. For α to reach 832 with zero successes, α must be incrementing on something else — most likely on SELECTION (the recommend path picking the variant) rather than on terminal SUCCESS (the variant's activity completing successfully).

Plausible mechanisms (each verifiable in code):
1. The Thompson write path in activity-api increments α at recommend-time, not at completion-time.
2. The write path increments α on `task.dispatched` events instead of `task.completed.success=true` events.
3. The `successful_executions` field is bug-affected — could be a separate counter that doesn't track what α is meant to.

**Cross-check with high-success templates:**

| Template | α | β | total | success |
|----------|---|---|-------|---------|
| `validator-dispatch` | 6224 | 1 | 6199 | 1.00 |
| `slot-binding` | 870.5 | 1 | 880 | 1.00 |

These look "right" — α ≈ total with 100% success. But this is consistent with EITHER (a) correct semantics where every selection succeeded, OR (b) buggy semantics where α counts selections and these templates happen to always succeed. **The data cannot distinguish these two hypotheses without verifying the write path.** If (b), then validator-dispatch's claimed 73/73 success the audit cycles have cited is the same kind of count, not an outcome measurement.

The full arc has been designing on top of measurements that may not reflect what they claim.
