## F-111: NEW — src/dist split creates audit verification traps (LOW)

The container's ias-executor-ts has a two-layer structure:
- `src/` — source TypeScript files, NOT updated on every sync
- `dist/` — compiled JavaScript, the actual runtime artifact

Current state:
- `src/engine.ts`: 487 lines, mtime May 25 02:34 UTC (pre-dates both 8cfa16f and 49bfb43)
- `dist/engine.js`: recompiled 2026-05-26 19:43:58 UTC, contains lifecycle:gap:classified at dist lines 78 and 125
- `src/resolvers/llm-prompt.ts`: 118 lines, mtime 17:56 UTC (9a7e0b2 = no lifecycle event)
- `dist/resolvers/llm-prompt.js`: compiled 18:04 UTC, contains lifecycle:llm:dispatched

Any audit probe that checks `src/` line counts or grep patterns will get stale/incorrect answers about the deployed state. The correct probe target is always `dist/`. Investigation-041's F-109 was a direct consequence of this ambiguity.

The deployment mechanism (sync + recompile into dist, but src may not be updated) is internally consistent but not documented in any way visible to an external auditor.
