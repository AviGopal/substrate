## The deciding code-fact

## What's in scope at the trace-build site

## What's missing on the typed carrier

`ExecutionTaskRecord` (`repos/ias-executor-ts/src/ontology.ts:139-151`) has no `inputShapes` / `outputShapes` / per-id shape fields. The push at `:244-255` has nothing to assign them to. The sink at `repos/ias-executor-ts/src/adapters/activity-api-trace-sink.ts:57-65` constructs with no store/runtime port — it sees only `ExecutionTrace`. So the wire body it builds at `:90-131` has no way to recover shapes that aren't on the trace.

## Why not Option B (sink-side plumbing)

Giving the sink access to the impulse store would require widening the `TraceSink` port contract (`repos/ias-executor-ts/src/ports.ts`) and threading runtime state through every host that constructs sinks. That's both **larger** than Option C and architecturally worse — the trace, not the sink, is the right carrier for shape provenance. The sink is a wire-translator; making it stateful adds coupling.
