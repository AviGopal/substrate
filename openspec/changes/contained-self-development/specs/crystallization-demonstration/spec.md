## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Self-development crystallizes a use case while components stay explorable
On system-filed work (no operator-authored goals, templates or posteriors), the system SHALL
show, with falsifiers registered before measurement: (1) the floor, a first instance of a
self-development use case reached by exploratory walk; (2) extraction, the ribosome minting a
template from that reached execution; (3) reuse, later similar instances selecting that pathway
with fewer consults and lower cost per reach at comparable reach; (4) adaptation, a near-miss
instance reusing the pathway with first- or last-mile walking instead of re-deriving it;
(5) flexibility, a variant of an underlying component explored alongside the pathway without
breaking it, promoted only on evidence.

#### Scenario: Reuse is cheaper than derivation
- **WHEN** the same use-case class is dispatched again after extraction
- **THEN** the pathway is selected, and consults per reach and cost per reach are lower than the first instance's, with reach not lower
