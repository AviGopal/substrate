In repos/development-vessel/src/config.ts, advertise the `composeOwnership` shape: in the discovery shapes list, add the entry `"composeOwnership",` immediately after the existing entry `"attempt_register",`. Measured: routes/impulses.ts serves composeOwnership, but a shape absent from this list is never registered with discovery, so a caller on another node cannot reach it through the federation transport. Change nothing else.
