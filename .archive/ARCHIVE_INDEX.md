1. **Historical Context**: Provides valuable insight into development evolution
2. **Decision Trail**: Documents why certain approaches were chosen
3. **Learning Reference**: Useful for understanding past challenges and solutions

5. **Safe Approach**: Preserves everything while reducing active clutter

**Recovery**: All archived files remain accessible at their archive locations. Git history also preserves all previous locations.
